# Name the two language settings with the terms translation apps use

The first two rows of the settings read 源语言 (source language) and 目标语言 (target language). In English they read From and To.

Why: the rows read 说话的语言 (the language being spoken) and 翻译成 (translate into), names made up for this app out of everyday words. The owner, seeing them, said that nobody writes that and that translation software names the pair from and to: a setting that other apps of the kind already have takes the name they give it, and an everyday paraphrase reads as something else.

Rejected: 从 and 到 in Chinese, word for word what the English has. Chinese translation apps do not label the pair that way.

Rejected: Source Language and Target Language in English. From and To is what the owner named, and the row's value says that it is a language.

Status of evidence: seen in an iPad simulator in both languages, 2026-11-25. Not run on a device. The owner has not seen the new names.
