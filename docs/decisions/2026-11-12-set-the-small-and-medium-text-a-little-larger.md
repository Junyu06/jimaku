# Set the small and medium text a little larger

A line of translation holds 54 columns at the small size and 46 at the medium size, twenty-seven and twenty-three Chinese characters. The original line above it holds 76 and 66 columns.

Why: the sizes went from 30, 24, and 22 columns to 40, 30, and 24, which the owner found still too large on an iPad, and then to 60, 50, and 40. Of those the owner kept the large one and reported the other two as a little too small. 54 and 46 lie between the two steps, nearer the smaller text, and keep three sizes about a sixth apart.

Cost accepted: the three sizes are now close together; a reader who wants text much larger than twenty characters to a line has no setting for it.

Status of evidence: the layout is covered by unit tests. These two sizes have not been seen on a device.
