# Write notices in the written terms apps of the kind use

The Chinese notices and the reasons shown beside a setting are written in the short written register that the system and translation apps use: 翻译不可用, 语言包未下载, 源语言与目标语言相同, 状态未知, 悬浮窗无法打开，请重试. Before, they were written as someone would say them: 翻译用不了, 语言包还没下载, 两边是同一种语言, 系统没有给出结果, 悬浮窗没能打开，请再点一次开始.

Why: the owner, seeing the two language rows named in everyday words, said to stop writing the interface that way and to use standard terms. Shown a list of the notices written the same way, with a replacement for each, he said to change them as listed. Four more that share a phrase with one on the list (the other two notices about the caption window, and the two about a language pack that is not downloaded or failed to download) were changed with them so that one thing keeps one wording; he has not seen those four.

Not changed: the page that says why the recording is needed, whose words the owner chose, and the labels about the recording, which he has read.

The English was not changed; it was not written in that manner.

Status of evidence: 460 unit tests pass; they check that each Chinese text they can read in the source still has its English. In an iPad simulator the heading over a running session's captions read 本次字幕; the notices need a failure to show and were not looked at on a screen. Not run on a device.
