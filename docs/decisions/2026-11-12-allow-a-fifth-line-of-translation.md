# Allow a fifth line of translation

The choice of lines for the main row goes up to five.

Why: the owner compared the window with another caption app that shows five lines of smaller text, and wanted to be able to read as much at once. With the smaller text sizes, five lines at the small size need a frame of 1200 by 438 pixels, a little taller than the smallest one.

Cost accepted: at the large size five lines make a taller window, 548 pixels for a width of 1200. The reader chooses.

Status of evidence: covered by unit tests, among them that every size with every number of lines fits its frame at full text size. The owner tried the choice of five on a physical iPad, at the text sizes of an earlier step, and reported it as what they would usually keep.
