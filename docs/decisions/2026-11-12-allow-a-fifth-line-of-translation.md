# Allow a fifth line of translation

The choice of lines for the main row goes up to five.

Why: the owner compared the window with another caption app that shows five lines of smaller text, and wanted to be able to read as much at once. With the smaller text sizes, five lines at the small size need a frame of 1200 by 462 pixels, which is still a wide, low window.

Cost accepted: at the medium and large sizes five lines make a tall window, 586 pixels and more for a width of 1200. The reader chooses.

Status of evidence: covered by unit tests, among them that every size with every number of lines fits its frame at full text size. Seen in an iPad simulator. Not run on a physical device.
