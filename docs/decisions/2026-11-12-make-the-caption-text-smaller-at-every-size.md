# Make the caption text smaller at every size

A line of translation now holds 40, 30, or 24 columns at the small, medium, and large size, where it held 30, 24, or 22. A wide character is two columns, so that is twenty, fifteen, or twelve Chinese characters. The original line above it holds 64, 52, or 46 columns. What was small is now medium, what was medium is now large, and the old large size is gone.

Why: the owner, on an iPad, found the medium and large sizes too large to use and the small one still much larger than the text of another caption app, whose window holds about twenty Chinese characters to a line. Smaller text puts more of what was said into the same window. The old large size was a twelfth narrower than the medium one, too little to be a choice of its own.

Rejected: a fourth, smaller size next to the three. The two larger ones were the ones the reader could not use.

Cost accepted:

- A reader who had chosen a size sees smaller text after this change, since the setting is stored by name.
- At the small size the original line is drawn at 35 pixels in a frame 1200 wide, which is small in a window that has been pinched down.
- With smaller text more lines fit the smallest frame: at the small size two to four lines of translation all use the 1200 by 400 frame, and only five need a taller one.

Status of evidence: seen in an iPad simulator, then on a physical iPad, where the owner reported every size as still two or three steps too large.
